About Craftwork

We design and build homes, and the things inside them, one detail at a time.

A design-build studio in Philadelphia led by Jayme Guokas. From new homes and whole-house renovations to the kitchens, built-ins and furniture that finish them, we see every project through ourselves.

How we work

What we do

One studio for the whole project: ground-up homes, whole-house renovations, kitchens and baths, built-ins, and furniture made in our own shop.

All services
New constructionGround-up homes, with us as general contractor. RenovationWhole-house and multi-room work in older homes worth keeping. Kitchens & bathsCabinetry, cast concrete and tile, drawn around how you live. Built-insLibraries, window seats and storage, scribed to the room. FurnitureTables, beds and seating, made to commission in our shop.
