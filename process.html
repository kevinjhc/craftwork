Process

Most projects move through these five stages, though no two follow them quite the same way. What stays constant is the team, and the attention to detail.

  1. 01 / 05

    Introduction

    A conversation about what you want to build, where and why. We visit the site, talk frankly about budget and timing, and tell you whether we’re the right fit.

    • Initial call
    • Site visit
    • Goals, budget & schedule

    1–2 weeks

  2. 02 / 05

    Design

    We develop the plan, materials and details with you through drawings, samples and mock-ups. Because we also build, every decision is priced and buildable as it’s made.

    • Concept & layout
    • Material & finish selections
    • Detailed drawings
    • Cabinetry & furniture design

    6–12 weeks, typical

  3. 03 / 05

    Pre-construction

    We turn the design into a fixed scope: detailed pricing, permits, a schedule and a lined-up team of trusted trades.

    • Itemized budget
    • Permits & approvals
    • Schedule & sequencing
    • Procurement

    4–8 weeks, typical

  4. 04 / 05

    Build

    Our crew runs the site, and cabinetry, concrete and furniture are made in parallel in our shop. You’ll have one point of contact and regular updates throughout.

    • Site management
    • Shop fabrication
    • Weekly updates
    • Quality walkthroughs

    Varies by scope

  5. 05 / 05

    Finish & care

    We install the final pieces, walk the house with you and handle every punch item. After you move in, we’re still a call away.

    • Installation & styling
    • Final walkthrough
    • Punch list
    • Care guide for materials

Questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Anything from a single piece of furniture to a new home. Most of our work is whole-house renovation, kitchens and baths, built-ins and custom furniture, in Philadelphia’s rowhouses, lofts and older homes. More on our services.

Where do you work?

Philadelphia, the surrounding towns and South Jersey. For furniture and commissioned pieces, get in touch wherever you are and we’ll talk it through.

I already have an architect. Can you build from their plans?

Yes. We work from your architect’s drawings, raise questions early, and bring our own cabinetry and details to the job. More on ways to work together.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on scope, finishes and the house itself. The ranges on our contact form are a good starting point. Before construction begins, you get an itemized budget so you know where every dollar goes.

How long does a project take?

Design usually takes six to twelve weeks and pre-construction four to eight. Build time varies with scope, and we’ll give you a schedule before any work starts.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. Permits and approvals are part of pre-construction, so you don’t have to deal with them yourself.

What happens after I get in touch?

We reply within a few business days to set up a call. If it sounds like a fit, we visit the site and talk through goals, budget and timing.
