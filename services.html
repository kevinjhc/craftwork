Services

We work closely with each client to shape spaces around the way they live. Whether it’s a new home, a single room or one piece of furniture, we start from what you need and build from there.

01

New Construction & General Contracting

We build new homes from the foundation up, and we act as general contractor, so the people who drew the plans are the people on site every day. One team is accountable for design, schedule, budget and finish.

Includes:

  • Site and feasibility review
  • Architectural coordination
  • Permitting & approvals
  • General contracting
  • Structural & envelope
  • Shop-built interiors
See projects Inquire more

02

Home Renovation

Whole-house and multi-room renovations, often of Philadelphia row homes, lofts and older houses with character worth keeping. We rework plans, restore what's good and add new work that sits comfortably beside it.

Includes:

  • Space planning
  • Structural changes
  • Additions & loft expansions
  • Historic detail matching
  • Stairs & millwork
  • Finish carpentry
See projects Inquire more

03

Kitchens & Baths

Kitchens designed around how you actually cook, and baths with the proportions of furniture. We draw, build and install the cabinetry ourselves and often pour the concrete counters and sinks in our shop.

Includes:

  • Ergonomic kitchen planning
  • Custom cabinetry
  • Cast concrete counters & sinks
  • Tile & stone
  • Fixtures & lighting
  • Powder rooms & primary baths
See projects Inquire more

04

Cabinetry & Built-ins

Libraries, window seats, closets, bars and storage walls, made in our shop and scribed to the room. The same hand that designs the piece builds it.

Includes:

  • Built-in libraries & shelving
  • Window seats & benches
  • Closets & dressing rooms
  • Bars & pantries
  • Commercial millwork
  • Hardware & finish selection
See projects Inquire more

05

Custom Furniture

Beds, tables, benches and seating made to commission from solid wood, reclaimed timber, steel and concrete. Some of these pieces are drawn for a particular house, and some go on to become the starting point for one.

Includes:

  • Dining & side tables
  • Beds & headboards
  • Benches & seating
  • Live-edge & slab work
  • Steel & mixed materials
  • Heirloom finishing
