About Us

Craftwork is a design-build studio in Philadelphia led by Jayme Guokas. We see every project through ourselves, from the first drawing to the last hinge.

The cabinetry, cast concrete and furniture are made by hand, so the details get the same care as the plan, down to the joinery. That covers new homes, whole-house renovations and everything that finishes them.
